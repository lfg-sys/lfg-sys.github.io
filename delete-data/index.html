Looking For Game

Delete your Looking For Game data

Looking For Game has optional accounts: you can sign in with Google to keep your wishlist safe, but you don't have to. Most of your data never leaves your phone, and you can delete the rest at any time.

Data on your phone

Your age range, platforms, genres, filters, wishlist and swipe history are stored only on your phone. To delete them, open Looking For Game, go to You, tap the version line at the bottom five times, then tap Reset everything. Uninstalling Looking For Game also deletes them.

Your account (if you signed in)

If you signed in with Google, we keep a copy of your wishlist, choices (age range, platforms, genres, filters) and the games you've swiped, linked to your Google email address. To delete it:

  • In Looking For Game, open You and tap Delete my account. The copy and your sign-in are erased straight away, and our private weekly backups drop it within 4 weeks.
  • Or, if you no longer have the app, email lfg@lfgame.co.uk from the Google address you signed in with, subject “Delete my Looking For Game account”. We delete everything within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Data we hold

If usage data is on, Looking For Game sends usage events and crash reports to our analytics service, linked to a random ID for your phone (not your name or email). To have them deleted:

  1. In Looking For Game, open You → Privacy and copy Your analytics ID. If usage data has never been on, we hold no usage data for you and there's nothing to delete.
  2. Email lfg@lfgame.co.uk with the subject “Delete my Looking For Game data” and paste in the ID.

We delete all usage events and crash reports for that ID within 30 days and reply to confirm. We then delete your email too. You can also switch usage data off at any time in You → Privacy so nothing more is sent.

Video reports

Reports of a video that won't play or isn't right contain the video, the reason, a random ID for your phone (separate from your analytics ID) and a scrambled code made from your internet connection's address, never your name or email. We can't link them to you, so they're kept to keep the game cards accurate and are deleted once the report has been dealt with or after 12 months.

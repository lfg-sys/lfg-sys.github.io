The Swapper

Facepalm Games · 2013

94% positive on Steam (7,600 reviews)

Create clones and swap between them to solve puzzles and gather orbs.

The Swapper is a short puzzle platformer where you must complete every puzzle and collect 124 orbs, in groups of 3 and 9 later on, to complete the game. The game has a tool which lets you create up to 4 clones and switch between them as long as you have a clear line of sight.

The Fall of Elena Temple

GrimTalin · 2023

93% positive on Steam (a few reviews)

Fall to grab coins, then undo back up before your undos run out.

An old-school puzzle platformer with monochrome pixel art. You fall from ledges to collect coins, then undo back to where you fell with the coins kept. Undos are limited, so each fall has to be planned to clear every room.

Icy Tower

Free Lunch Design AB · 2001

93% positive on Steam (227 reviews)

Jump from step to step up a tall tower, chaining combos as you climb higher.

Icy Tower is a single-player platform action game in which a character climbs a tower by jumping onto the steps of a staircase inside it. Players can create custom characters, unlock different floor types and earn rewards for combos. Replays can be saved and shared with friends.

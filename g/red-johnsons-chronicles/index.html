Red Johnson's Chronicles

Lexis Numerique · 2011

Investigate as private eye Red Johnson, who has a price on his head.

Red Johnson's Chronicles is a 2011 puzzle-based investigation game from Lexis Numerique, set in a noir world. Detective Red Johnson of Metropolis City has a price on his head and an abducted brother, and you use wit, persuasion and sometimes fists to solve the case.

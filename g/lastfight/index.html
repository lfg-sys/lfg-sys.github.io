Pick up any object and fight in 2v2 3D brawls across eight stages.

Lastfight is a 2v2 3D fighting game from Piranaking with characters inspired by the comic book Lastman. You can pick up objects in its eight stages, and play in local multiplayer for up to four players or in a one-player story mode.

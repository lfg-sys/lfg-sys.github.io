Explore Marie's room and piece together a school friendship through memory.

This solo indie adventure is a short exploration game about an unconventional friendship between two classmates, told through memories. The player sees Marie's room as Kelsey remembers it from twenty years earlier, and the story asks what happened between the two of them.

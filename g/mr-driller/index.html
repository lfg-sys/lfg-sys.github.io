Namco · 1999

Drill down through coloured blocks before your air runs out.

An arcade puzzle game from Namco, first released in 1999. You drill down through layers of coloured blocks while watching a limited air supply, and blocks vanish when four or more of the same colour connect, so each move needs quick thinking.

Tomcat Alley

The Code Monkeys · 1994

Fly an F-14 Tomcat and dogfight MiGs in a full-motion video game.

Tomcat Alley is a 1994 full-motion video game for the Sega CD and one of Sega's last TruVideo titles. You fly an F-14 against a renegade Soviet commander threatening the United States, and your choices lead to many different outcomes.

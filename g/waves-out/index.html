Waves Out!

Crevice · 2021

Use magnetic powers to pull coloured spheres into the goal holes.

A competitive arcade game in which you control Magnetin, a small metal character with magnetic powers. You attract spheres of your colour into goal holes to score, using special powers that appear around the arena. Play against a second player or in Time Trial.

Side Decide

Blue Volcano Studio · 2021

Roll a cube across the board, matching faces to the blocks below.

Side Decide is a 2021 puzzle game from Blue Volcano Studio. You roll a cube to the end of each board, but can only roll onto a block with the matching face, while circles let you rotate and open new paths.

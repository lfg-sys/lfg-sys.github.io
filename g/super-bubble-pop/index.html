Super Bubble Pop

Runecraft · 2002

Pop rows of coloured bubbles as a DJ in a music-driven puzzle game.

Super Bubble Pop is a video game of the puzzle genre released in 2002 by Runecraft. The Player is able to choose from 5 DJ poppers, including 2 unlockable ones, along with 4 grooving soundtracks. They are faced with rows of advancing bubbles of different colors. They can launch bubbles of their own.

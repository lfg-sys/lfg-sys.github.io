Mortal Kombat Trilogy

Midway Games · 1996

62 / 100 critic score

Fight with added characters and stages, then finish rivals with a long Brutality combo.

Mortal Kombat Trilogy is a fighting game that repeats the gameplay and story of Mortal Kombat 3. It adds characters and stages from three other arcade games, along with some new fighters. During a match, players build an Aggressor meter that temporarily makes their character faster and stronger.

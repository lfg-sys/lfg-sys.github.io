Seasons of the Samurai

Francis Malka · 2020

Change the season and time of day to clear a samurai's path.

A logic puzzle game set across the eight provinces of Japan. You change the season and the time of day to rearrange each level and give a samurai safe passage onward. New mechanics appear as you go, and the puzzles test both logic and memory.

Slay

Sean O'Connor · 2009

91% positive on Steam (under 1,000 reviews)

Conquer an island hex by hex, balancing a stronger army against its upkeep.

A remake of the turn-based strategy game Slay with new graphics and sound. You conquer an island by buying men and capturing enemy hexagons. Combining men makes stronger units that cost more to maintain, and cutting enemy territory in half can bankrupt part of it.

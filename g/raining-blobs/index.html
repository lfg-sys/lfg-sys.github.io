Raining Blobs

Endi Milojkoski · 2016

86% positive on Steam (101 reviews)

Match falling blocks and use stars to clear linked groups.

Raining Blobs is a fast arcade puzzle game from Endi Milojkoski, released in 2016. Blocks of the same colour link together when matched, and matching blocks with stars destroys the linked groups. It can be played alone or with others.

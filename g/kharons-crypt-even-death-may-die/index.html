Kharon's Crypt: Even Death May Die

Hebi Lee · 2022

95% positive on Steam (106 reviews)

Possess your enemies' bodies to escape a crypt as Death himself.

A non-linear dungeon crawler styled after the Game Boy Color, full of puzzles and riddles. You play Kharon, thought to be Death, sealed in a crypt by a mad king. You fly, pass through objects, stun enemies and possess their bodies, such as a bat or a skeleton, to escape.

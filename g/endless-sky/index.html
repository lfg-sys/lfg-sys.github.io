Endless Sky

Michael Zahniser · 2015

92% positive on Steam (7,200 reviews)

Trade and fight across space, from a weak shuttle to a whole fleet.

Endless Sky is a free, open-source 2D space trading and combat game inspired by the Escape Velocity series. You start with a modest shuttle, cargo ship or fighter and work your way up to an upgraded flagship or a large fleet.

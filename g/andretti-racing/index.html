Andretti Racing

High Score Entertainment · 1996

Race NASCAR or Indy cars on 16 tracks or through a full career.

Andretti Racing is a 1996 arcade racing game in which you drive NASCAR or Indy cars on 16 tracks. You can race single events or a full career, with commentary from Derek Daly and Bob Jenkins, and pit-crew mistakes or breakdowns can affect races.

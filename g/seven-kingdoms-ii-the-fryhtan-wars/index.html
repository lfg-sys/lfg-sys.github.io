Seven Kingdoms II: The Fryhtan Wars

Enlight Software Limited · 1999

80 / 100 critic score

Lead humans or demonic Fryhtans in medieval real-time strategy battles.

Seven Kingdoms II is a 2D RTS that took place during the medieval ages. It is notable for its two vastly different playable civilization types: Humans and Fryhtans (demons). It was lauded for its uniqueness and variety of gameplay, however, it failed to compete commercially against Age of Empires 2.

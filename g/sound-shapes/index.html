Queasy Games · 2012

Run and jump through 2D levels where your every move makes the music.

A side-scrolling platformer that is part game and part instrument: your actions build the music. The campaign mixes art by Pixeljam, Capy and Superbrothers with music from Jim Guthrie, I Am Robot and Proud and Deadmau5, and you can also compose your own levels.

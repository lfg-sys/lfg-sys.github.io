Boost Beast

Arzest · 2017

Match animal blocks to summon friends that fight off waves of zombies.

"BOOST BEAST" is a defense puzzle game where players defeat the invading horde of zombies by clearing blocks and summoning animals. The controls are simple. Match 3 animal blocks of the same color to summon your cute animal friends and defeat the incoming waves of zombies.

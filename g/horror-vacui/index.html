Horror Vacui

Christoph Frey · 2012

Explore the memories of a patient in a coma, with no enemies to fight.

Horror Vacui is an experimental game by Christoph Frey, made in 2012, set in the mind of a patient in a coma. You explore rooms and objects, and the fragments of memory you find change those that follow, so it is meant to be played more than once.

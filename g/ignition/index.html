Ignition

Unique Development Studios · 1997

85% positive on Steam (under 1,000 reviews)

Race buses, beetles and trucks in a top-down racer.

Ignition is an action packed racing game viewed from above and slightly behind the player. Initially there are 5 tracks available, with practice and single race modes as well as full championship at 3 levels - winning each of the first 2 levels unlocks an extra track.

Sky Target

SEGA AM1 · 1995

Shoot down anything that moves and pick your route through branching stages.

Sky Target is a third-person arcade flight shooter from SEGA, similar to After Burner. Players pick one of four planes, clear stages by shooting enemies and choose branching paths between them, relying on a life bar instead of multiple lives.

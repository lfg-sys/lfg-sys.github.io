VR Soccer

Gremlin Interactive · 1995

68 / 100 critic score

Play football in one of the first sports games with full 3D graphics.

VR Soccer, known as Actua Soccer in Europe and Japan, is a 1995 football game from Gremlin Interactive. It was the first sports game with a full 3D engine, and three Sheffield Wednesday players were motion-captured for its animations.

Jurassic Park: Operation Genesis (often abbreviated JP:OG or simply JPOG) is a park-building game that allows players to create their own version of Jurassic Park. It is reminiscent of games like SimCity and Zoo Tycoon. Parks can earn stars by entertaining visitors.

BomberCin 2

Kousaku-P / NovaWare · 2021

Climb ladders and use bombs to clear out nasty bugs as a little chick.

A homage to early arcade and NES games such as Mario Bros. and Bomberman. The player is Cin, a helmeted chick who cannot jump, and must climb ladders and use an endless supply of bombs to defend against and defeat an infestation of bugs.

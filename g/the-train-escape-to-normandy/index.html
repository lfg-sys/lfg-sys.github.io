The Train: Escape to Normandy

Artech Digital Entertainments, Inc. · 1987

Drive a hijacked train out of Nazi Germany while shooting down fighter planes.

The Train: Escape to Normandy is a World War II action game loosely based on the film The Train. Players run a hijacked train out of Nazi Germany, keeping its speed and pressure in check, manning anti-aircraft guns and giving cover fire at stations.

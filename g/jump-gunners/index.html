Jump Gunners

Use your guns' recoil to jump around arenas in couch combat for four.

Jump Gunners is a 2017 couch combat game for up to four players from NerdRage Studios. You use gun recoil to jump around, and there are several modes, more than 20 weapons, over 80 destructible arenas, custom battles and solo challenges.

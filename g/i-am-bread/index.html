I am Bread

Bossa Studios · 2014

78% positive on Steam (6,000 reviews)

Guide a slice of bread to toast while keeping it clear of water and the floor.

I am Bread is an action-adventure puzzle platformer in which the player controls a slice of bread through levels based on the days of the week. Each level aims to toast the bread, but touching objects, water or the floor lowers its edible meter.

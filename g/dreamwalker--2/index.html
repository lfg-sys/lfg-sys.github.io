Dreamwalker

Prisma Games · 2023

Give sleepwalkers traits so they reach their beds safely.

A puzzle game made for the Tirana Game Jam on the theme of dreams, mixing ideas from Lemmings and Monument Valley. You guide sleepwalkers to bed by handing them traits that help them cross each level.

Gravity Mastery

RedZyrro · 2021

Change the direction of gravity to pass a series of tests.

Gravity Mastery is a 2021 puzzle game from RedZyrro where you decide which way is down. You shift gravity between six directions to complete a series of logical trials set by The Adviser, the current master of gravity.

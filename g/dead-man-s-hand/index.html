Dead Man's Hand

Human Head Studios · 2004

Shoot outlaws and play poker in a Wild West shooter.

The game is somewhat unusual in that between levels, the player plays poker for extra health and ammunition. The character is armed with 4 default weapons; a knife, a pistol, a rifle and a shotgun. Other weapons that can be found are TNT and Whisky bombs.

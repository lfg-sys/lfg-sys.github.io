Party Crashers

Giant Margarita · 2018

88% positive on Steam (a few reviews)

Race on one screen and knock rivals out until one car is left.

Party Crashers is an arcade combat racing game for several players. Everyone races on a single screen, cars that fall too far behind or off the track are knocked out, and weapons on the track help eliminate rivals. Its rules can be customised in detail.

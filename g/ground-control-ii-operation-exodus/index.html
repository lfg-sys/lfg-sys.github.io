Ground Control II: Operation Exodus

Massive Entertainment · 2004

89% positive on Steam (333 reviews)

Command troops in Ground Control II.

Like the original, Ground Control II is not a standard base-building RTS game. There is no technical upgrade tree, and no mining for gold, crystals, or any other random mineral to be used as a monetary source. New units are acquired by spending acquisition points which collect throughout the battle.

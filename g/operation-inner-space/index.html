Operation: Inner Space

Software Dynamics · 1994

Fly a spaceship inside a computer to recover lost icons.

An action game in which you pilot a spaceship into a computer's Inner Space to recover icons and resources set loose by an invasion and destroy the Inner Demon. You pick a ship from one of six teams, which shapes how other craft treat you, and can race for icons.

Percy the Penguin Saves Christmas

PixelDough · 2017

Stun rogue toys with snowballs and wrap them up as presents.

A platformer in the style of old arcade games such as Mario Bros. and Bubble Bobble. You throw snowballs to stun rogue Christmas toys, then touch them to wrap them as presents, clearing each level for one or two local players.

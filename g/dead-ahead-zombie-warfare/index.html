Dead Ahead: Zombie Warfare

Mobirate · 2017

Defend your bus and deploy survivors to smash through the zombies' barricade.

A zombie strategy game in which you defend a bus and try to destroy the enemy barricade by sending in units and using abilities. Units cost courage, which builds up over time, while abilities and some units need rage, earned by killing enemies.

Change shape to beat enemies in a rock-paper-scissors platformer.

Flat Kingdom is a platformer with puzzles and metroidvania elements, from Fat Panda Games, released in 2016. Instead of attacking directly, you touch enemies while in the right shape, as each shape beats its opposite: sharp triangles defeat soft circles, for example.

World of Guns: Gun Disassembly

Noble Empire Corp. · 2014

84% positive on Steam (20k reviews)

Take apart and reassemble real firearms in 3D, against the clock if you like.

A 3D simulator and interactive encyclopaedia of firearms and other machines, from pistols to tanks. You can look inside a gun, cut it in half, fire it in slow motion and strip it down to its smallest parts before putting it back together, against the clock if you wish.

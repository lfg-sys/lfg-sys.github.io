Pick clubs from your bag and play golf in VR on thousands of courses.

A VR golf game built on HB Studios' The Golf Club, with over 130,000 procedurally and user-generated courses. You choose clubs from an interactive bag, plan each hole with a yardage booklet and get shot information from a golf watch.

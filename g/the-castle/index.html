The Castle

ASCII Corporation · 1985

Explore a huge castle to rescue a princess in an early map-based platformer.

The Castle is a 1985 platform adventure from ASCII Corporation. You explore a castle to rescue a princess; it was very large for its time, with a map system similar to later Metroid and Castlevania games. It was followed by Castle Excellent.

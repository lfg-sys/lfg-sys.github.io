Rogue Legacy

Abstraction Games · 2013

92% positive on Steam (19k reviews)

Fight on one heir at a time, each with random traits, when the last one dies.

A platform action game about a family line. You play one character at a time, and when they die you choose an heir to carry on. Traits are randomised each generation from 35 possibilities, so each character plays differently.

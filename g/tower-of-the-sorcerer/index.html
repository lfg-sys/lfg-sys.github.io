Tower of the Sorcerer

Caravan Circle · 1994

Climb a tower floor by floor, fighting for keys to rescue the princess.

A cross between a puzzle game and a role-playing game. To save the princess the hero climbs the floors of a tower, fighting enemies and collecting keys to open doors, and gathering items that raise attack, defence and health. The levels are fixed rather than random.

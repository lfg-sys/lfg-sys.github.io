Densha de GO! Professional

Taito GM R&D Sect. #2 · 1999

Drive trains along the lines of the first two games and the Toukaidou Line.

A train simulation game by Taito featuring the lines from the first two Densha de Go games and parts of the Toukaidou Line in the Kantou area. It is less strict than earlier games, adding a panel that maps the track ahead and its speed limits, so less memorisation is needed.

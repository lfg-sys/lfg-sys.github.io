Fight through random dungeon floors as Isaac, stacking strange items into odd combos.

A top-down roguelike action game and a rebuilt remake of the 2011 original. You guide Isaac through randomly generated dungeon floors, firing tears at monsters and collecting items that change his abilities. Death ends the run, and each one brings new layouts, items and bosses.

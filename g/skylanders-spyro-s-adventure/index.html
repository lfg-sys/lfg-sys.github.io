Place toy figures on a portal to bring them into the game and play as them.

The first game in the Skylanders series. It is played with toy figures that you put on a Portal of Power, which reads each figure's tag and brings that character into the game. The voice cast includes Josh Keaton and Kevin Michael Richardson.

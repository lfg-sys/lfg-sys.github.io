Privacy notice

LFG – Looking For Game, an app for finding games by swiping through gameplay. This is the same notice shown in the app under You → Privacy notice. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Who runs LFG

LFG – Looking For Game is a trading name of Rung App Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17278974). LFG decides how your data is used (the data controller). Contact: lfg@lfgame.co.uk.

What stays on your phone

Your age range, platforms, genres, filters (including content filters), wishlist and the games you have swiped are saved on this phone. Unless you sign in (below), they are not sent anywhere.

Your account (optional)

If you sign in with Google (on the You tab), LFG keeps a copy of your wishlist, your age range, platforms, genres and filters, and the games you have swiped in our database (Supabase), linked to your Google account's email address and ID, so you can get them back if you reinstall LFG or use a new phone. Only you can see or change them. Google tells us only your name, email address and Google ID; we don't get your password or anything else from your Google account.

Sign out at any time. "Delete my account" on the You tab erases the copy and your sign-in straight away (our private weekly backups drop it within 4 weeks); what is on your phone stays. If you don't sign in, nothing changes: everything stays on your phone only.

Usage data (you can turn it off)

Unless you turn it off, LFG sends usage events to PostHog, our analytics service (hosted in the EU): which games you swipe or wishlist, how long a video played, which buttons and tabs you use, your filter settings, and when the app is opened or closed. If the app crashes or hits an error, a crash report (what went wrong in the app’s code, and on which screen) is sent the same way.

Each event carries a random ID created for this phone, plus the app version, phone model and operating system. We do not send your name, email or contacts, and location lookup from your internet address is turned off.

If you told LFG you are 13–17, usage data is off unless you switch it on yourself in You → Privacy. This data is used only to see how LFG is used and to improve it. It is not sold or used for advertising. Turn it off on the first-launch screen or any time in You → Privacy; nothing more is sent after that. "Reset everything" also replaces the random ID.

Who LFG is for

LFG is for players aged 13 and over. If you tell LFG you are 13–17, games rated 18 are never shown to you. Content filters on the You tab can hide 18-rated, horror, strong gore or sexual content for anyone. These use the age ratings published for each game (PEGI first).

Video problems

When a video won't play, LFG tells our database (Supabase) which video failed and the error code, so it can be replaced. When you report a video, the report also includes the reason you chose, a random ID for this phone, and a scrambled code made from your internet connection's address (the address itself is never stored), so one person's reports count once and a video is only removed after reports from several different people.

Sponsored games

Some games may be marked Sponsored: a developer paid for them to be shown, at most once in every 8 cards and only if they fit your filters. To count fairly what they pay for, when a sponsored game is shown, saved or its store button tapped, LFG tells our database (Supabase) the campaign number and which of those happened. Nothing else is sent: no phone ID, no other games and no settings. Each internet connection is counted once a day, using a scrambled code made from its address that changes every day (the address itself is never stored), and those codes are deleted after 90 days. This count happens even with usage data turned off, because it only records the sponsored card, not you.

Feedback

Send feedback (on the You tab) opens your own email app with a message to us, including the app version, phone model and Android version, plus your analytics ID if usage data is on. You can read and edit it all before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send the email. We use your email address only to reply.

YouTube

LFG uses YouTube API Services to show videos. Videos play in YouTube’s own player, and YouTube may collect data as you watch. By watching videos in LFG you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s Privacy Policy applies.

Questions

For questions about your data, or to ask us to delete it, email lfg@lfgame.co.uk. Include the analytics ID from You → Privacy if you want us to find your usage data.

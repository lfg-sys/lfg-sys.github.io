How it works

Three seconds to know if a game is for you.

Store pages and trailers show a game at its best. LFG shows you what playing it is actually like.

Watch

Every card opens with real gameplay, muted and ready to go, so you see the game being played rather than a sales pitch.

Swipe

Skip it or save it. Each choice shapes the rest of your deck, so the more you swipe, the better it gets.

Play

Saved games wait on your wishlist with the best price we can find. One tap takes you to the store.

Features

Built for people who'd rather play than scroll.

Gameplay first

Videos of people actually playing the game, with trailers there too.

Only what you can play

Pick your platforms, genres, price and play time. Your deck only shows games that fit.

The best price

Prices from the stores you use, in your currency, with sales flagged so you know when to buy.

A wishlist that follows you

Your saved games stay on your phone. Sign in with Google if you'd like them back on a new one.

Coming soon

Upcoming games with their release dates, so the ones you're waiting for don't pass you by.

Your data, your choice

No account needed, and usage data is only shared if you turn it on.
